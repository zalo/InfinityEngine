How the toggle works

Each demo starts in Standard mode (PlayCanvas default: variable-rate Ammo.js physics). Toggle to Deterministic mode to switch to fixed-timestep (60Hz) physics with render interpolation. Same Ammo.js engine, but physics steps at exactly 1/60s regardless of frame rate — essential for replays and rollback networking. The toggle calls app.enableDeterministicSimulation() from our fork.
